DATA PRIVACY & SECURITY

Privacy Policy & Trust Manifesto

Your business billing data belongs 100% to your company. We enforce strict multi-tenant isolation, row-level database security, and zero data sharing.

🔒

Strict Company Isolation

Every customer list, catalogue rate, and invoice is partitioned by company. Your records are never merged or visible to other companies.

🛡️

Encrypted Cloud & Local Mode

All cloud traffic is protected by TLS/HTTPS and Supabase Row-Level Security. Offline/Guest data stays strictly on your local browser.

📄

100% Data Ownership

You retain full intellectual property over all your invoices. Export your invoices and client records as PDF or print them anytime.

1 Information We Collect & Store

We only collect and store information essential for generating valid GST-compliant invoices:

  • Business Details: Company trade name, registered GSTIN, address, phone number, and custom uploaded logo.
  • Financial & Banking: Bank account numbers, IFSC codes, and payment info printed on your invoice sheets.
  • Customer Directory: Client names, phone numbers, and addresses you save for billing convenience.
  • Invoices & Items: Quantities, tax rates, line items, and invoice history.

2 Zero Third-Party Monetization

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your customer lists, sales figures, or business records with advertisers, marketers, or data brokers.

3 Data Rights & Support

You have the full right to edit your business details, delete records, export invoices as PDF, and request account deletion.

For any privacy inquiries or assistance, reach us at support@skonetech.com.

1 Commitment to Data Privacy

SK One Tech Billing is built to provide professional invoicing while keeping your business confidentiality top priority. We operate under strict multi-tenant isolation principles, meaning each company's data is 100% isolated and never merged with another company.

2 Information We Collect

We collect only the business data necessary to prepare and print your invoices:

  • Company Identity: Business name, logo, address, GSTIN, and contact details.
  • Banking Information: Account number, IFSC code, and payment details for customer remittance.
  • Customer & Inventory Records: Customer directory, product/service catalogue items, rates, and tax percentages.
  • Invoices: Recorded invoice numbers, line items, timestamps, and payment statuses.

3 Local-First / Guest Workspace

When using Instant / Local Mode, all your invoice records and company profiles are stored locally in your browser storage and are never uploaded to any remote server without explicit login.

4 Data Security & Encryption

All cloud communications are encrypted via HTTPS/TLS 1.3. Cloud databases are guarded with Row-Level Security (RLS) policies guaranteeing that only authenticated members of your company can access your records.

5 Contact & Support

For questions or assistance regarding your company data, contact SK One Tech Support at support@skonetech.com.
